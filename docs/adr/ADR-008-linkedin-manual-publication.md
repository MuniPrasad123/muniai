# ADR-008: Manual LinkedIn publication

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-07-21

## Context

Publishing professional content is a high-impact reputational action. Automated publication also expands credentials, platform-policy, and prompt-injection risk.

## Decision

MuniAI may help draft and revise LinkedIn content locally, but it will not publish to LinkedIn. The user must manually copy, review, and publish content through LinkedIn's own interface.

## Consequences

Publication remains unmistakably user-controlled and no LinkedIn write credential is needed. The workflow has a deliberate manual step and cannot provide automated posting or scheduling.
